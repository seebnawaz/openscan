OpenScan turns your camera into a document scanner — fully free, fully on-device,
no account, no ads, and no watermark on your exports.

Features:
* Live edge detection with a real-time outline as you aim
* Automatic perspective correction (de-skew) to a flat page
* Enhance filters: Original, Grayscale, Black & White, and Magic Color
* Multi-page documents — reorder and delete pages
* Export to PDF with no watermark, ever
* Searchable-PDF OCR (on-device, Tesseract)
* Everything runs locally — your documents never leave your phone

OpenScan uses only free and open-source components (OpenCV for image processing,
Tesseract for OCR). No Google Play Services required.
